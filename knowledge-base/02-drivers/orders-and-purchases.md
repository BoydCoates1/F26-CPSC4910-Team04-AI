# Orders and Purchases

## Overview
Drivers use points to request products from the catalog maintained by their selected sponsor. The exact current options and status are shown in the website.

Do not make claims about fulfillment or delivery beyond the information displayed for the order.

## Before placing an order
- Confirm that the product belongs to your sponsor's catalog.
- Review the product description.
- Check current availability.
- Confirm the points required.
- Make sure your available point balance is sufficient.

## Placing an order
1. Sign in as a driver and choose **Products**.
2. Select a product and choose **Buy** to add it to your cart.
3. Open **Cart**, review the items and quantities, then use the available order action.
4. Review the confirmation and any displayed order information.

## Reviewing order status
Open **Dashboard** and choose **Past Orders** to review previous orders. The page shows order statuses such as pending, ordered, or rejected. Do not assume that tracking information is available.

## Updating or cancelling an order
The actions available depend on order status. A pending order can be changed. A refund action appears only when the website considers the order eligible.

1. Open **Dashboard** and choose **Past Orders**.
2. Find the pending order and use the available change action, or choose the refund action if it appears.
3. Read the confirmation carefully before proceeding.

The interface states that a refund is available within 24 hours until the sponsor places the order. The confirmation says that refunding permanently deletes the order and its points history and returns the points. Do not promise a refund outside the eligibility and confirmation shown in the live site.

## Points and refunds
For refunds, read the current confirmation, which describes the points returned and permanent deletion of the order and its points history. If the resulting balance does not match the confirmation, contact the sponsor.

## Missing or delayed orders
Check **Past Orders** for the current status. If it does not answer your question, contact the sponsor or program support using the route provided by the program.
