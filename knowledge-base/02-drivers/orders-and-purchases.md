# Orders and Purchases

## Overview
Drivers use points to request products from the catalog maintained by their sponsor. The website is expected to check the current product price and availability when the product is added to the purchase set or cart.

Products are sold and delivered through a third party. The program records the points-based order and displays its status; the third party handles fulfillment.

## Before placing an order
- Confirm that the product belongs to your sponsor's catalog.
- Review the product description.
- Check current availability.
- Confirm the points required.
- Make sure your available point balance is sufficient.

## Placing an order
1. Sign in as a driver.
2. Open your sponsor's product catalog.
3. Select an available product.
4. Review the current product information and points required.
5. Follow the website's order or checkout steps.
6. Review the order confirmation and save any order reference shown.

## Reviewing order status
Drivers are intended to be able to review the status of their purchases. Open the orders, purchases, or order history area and select the relevant order.

The website may display information such as order date, product, status, and a tracking number when one is available.

## Updating or cancelling an order
The project requirements say drivers can cancel or update purchases. The options available may depend on the current order status and third-party fulfillment stage.

1. Open the order history.
2. Select the order.
3. Check whether an update or cancellation action is offered.
4. Review any confirmation message before submitting.

Do not promise that every order can be cancelled or changed. If the action is unavailable, contact the sponsor or program support. A third-party provider may already be processing the product.

## Points and refunds
Do not assume when points are returned after a cancellation. The live website's confirmation and program policy are authoritative. If the returned balance does not match the displayed cancellation result, contact the sponsor.

## Missing or delayed orders
Check the order status and tracking information first. For shipment delays, delivery address changes, damaged products, or missing packages, follow the support route identified by the website or third-party provider.
