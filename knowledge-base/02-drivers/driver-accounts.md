# Driver Accounts and Profiles

## What a driver account is
A driver account lets a participant access the program, review their point balance, browse the catalog associated with their sponsor, and manage eligible purchases.

A driver is affiliated with one sponsor company at a time under the project requirements.

## Getting an account or joining a sponsor
A driver may use the driver-application process to request affiliation with a sponsor. The sponsor reviews the request and decides whether to accept or reject it.

Submitting an application should not be described as guaranteed acceptance. If an application is pending, the driver should wait for the sponsor's decision or contact the sponsor through the contact method provided by the program.

## Signing in
Use the account identifier and password requested by the website. The system should identify the user's role from the account rather than asking the user to choose a role at login.

## Updating a profile
1. Sign in as a driver.
2. Open the profile or account settings area.
3. Review the displayed information.
4. Edit the fields the website allows you to change.
5. Save the changes and confirm that the updated information is displayed.

## Changing a password
Use the account or security settings if the website provides a password-change option. If you cannot sign in, use the password-reset process described in `05-support/login-and-password-help.md`.

Never share your current password with a sponsor user, administrator, or chatbot.

## Account access questions
If a driver cannot see a catalog, point balance, or order history, first confirm that they are signed in to the correct account and that the sponsor relationship is active. If the problem continues, contact the sponsor or program support.
