# Driver Accounts and Profiles

## What a driver account is
A driver account lets a participant access the program, review their point balance, browse the catalog associated with their sponsor, and manage eligible purchases.

The dashboard can show multiple company associations and allows a driver to switch the currently viewed company. The company currently selected affects the company-specific information shown; do not assume every company association is active or approved.

## Getting an account or joining a sponsor
A driver can start or view an application from the company section of **Dashboard**. Choose **Apply To Company** if not associated with a company, or **Apply To Another Company** if the dashboard already lists a company. Select an available company and submit the application. The sponsor reviews it and decides whether to accept or reject it.

Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance. A pending application is shown on the dashboard. The dashboard also offers an application-cancellation flow with a required response reason; use the live confirmation and message before cancelling.

The registration form currently offers **Driver** and **Sponsor** account types. For a driver, it can show a sponsor selector and CDL upload. Exact required fields depend on the current form.

## Signing in
The current **Log in** form asks for a username and password. It does not ask the user to select a role.

## Updating a profile
1. Sign in and open the profile menu by selecting your username in the main navigation.
2. Choose **Profile**, then choose **Edit**.
3. Update the fields the page allows and submit the form.
4. Verify the updated information on **Profile**.

The profile displays username, email, name, phone, and address. The edit form controls which fields can be changed.

## Changing a password
When signed in, open **Profile** and use the password/account editing options shown there. If you cannot sign in, use **Forgot your password?** on the login page as described in `05-support/login-and-password-help.md`.

Never share your current password with a sponsor user, administrator, or chatbot.

## Account access questions
If a driver cannot see a catalog, point balance, or order history, first confirm that they are signed in to the correct account and that the sponsor relationship is active. If the problem continues, contact the sponsor or program support.
