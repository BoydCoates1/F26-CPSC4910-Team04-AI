# Browsing Reward Products

## Which products can a driver see?
Drivers browse the catalog associated with their selected sponsor company. Each sponsor maintains its own catalog. A driver should not assume that products listed by another sponsor are available to them.

The project requirements limit catalog products to G- or PG-rated content. The actual rating or information source may not be visible for every product; do not infer a rating or product detail that is not shown.

## Product information
Depending on the provider and product, the catalog may show:
- Product name.
- Description.
- Image.
- Current price or points required.
- Availability.
- Other product details.

Product prices and availability can change. Use the information currently displayed by the website.

## Browsing
1. Sign in as a driver and choose **Products**.
2. Search or sort the catalog shown for your selected sponsor.
3. Select a product to review its details and points cost.
4. Choose **Buy** to add it to your cart, then review the cart before submitting an order.

## Why a product may be unavailable
A product may be unavailable because its catalog information changed or the product is no longer offered. Follow the availability information shown by the website.

## Product questions
For product specifications, warranties, shipping, returns, or fulfillment, the third-party seller or provider may be the authoritative source. The program website may display provider information but does not necessarily control fulfillment.
