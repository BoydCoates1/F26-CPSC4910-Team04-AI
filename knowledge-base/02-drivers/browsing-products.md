# Browsing Reward Products

## Which products can a driver see?
Drivers browse the catalog associated with their sponsor. Each sponsor maintains its own catalog. A driver should not assume that products listed by another sponsor are available to them.

The project requirements limit catalog products to G- or PG-rated content. Product information is intended to come from a publicly accessible third-party API.

## Product information
Depending on the provider and product, the catalog may show:
- Product name.
- Description.
- Image.
- Current price or points required.
- Availability.
- Other product details.

Product prices and availability can change. The website is expected to refresh this information through its product provider, including when an item is added to a driver's purchase set or cart.

## Browsing
1. Sign in as a driver.
2. Open the product catalog or rewards area.
3. Browse or search the products shown.
4. Open a product to review its details.
5. Check the points required and availability before continuing.

## Why a product may be unavailable
A product may be unavailable because the third-party provider has changed its inventory, the product is no longer listed, or the catalog information has refreshed. A product visible earlier may not remain available when the driver proceeds to checkout.

## Product questions
For product specifications, warranties, shipping, returns, or fulfillment, the third-party seller or provider may be the authoritative source. The program website may display provider information but does not necessarily control fulfillment.
