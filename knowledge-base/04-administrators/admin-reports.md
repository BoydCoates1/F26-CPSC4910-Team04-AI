# Administrator Reports

## Current Sales Report
The administrator dashboard provides **Sales Reports**. The **Sales Report** page has sponsor and date filters, a Summary/Detailed choice, and a PDF download.

## Running a report
1. Sign in as an administrator and open **Dashboard**.
2. Choose **Sales Reports**.
3. Select the sponsor, date range, and Summary or Detailed view.
4. Review the results and use the PDF download if needed.

The project brief describes other report types, including Sales by Driver and Sponsor Invoice, but the current Sales Report interface does not confirm those reports or their calculations. Do not claim that they are available or calculate a fee from the brief.

Administrators can open **Audit Logs** from the dashboard. For its filters and scope, see `audit-logs.md`.

## Report interpretation
Verify the selected sponsor, date range, and report view before interpreting results. Do not assume a report's calculations beyond what the page displays.
