# Viewing the Website as Another User

## What impersonation means
The project requirements specify that sponsor users and administrators can assume another user's identity to view the website from that user's point of view and perform operations that the represented user is allowed to perform.

- A sponsor user may assume the identity of a driver.
- An administrator may assume the identity of a driver or sponsor user.

The purpose is to help authorized staff understand or assist with a user-facing issue.

## Important permission boundaries
When acting as another user, the available actions should follow the represented user's permissions. Impersonation should not be treated as permission to bypass the application's role restrictions.

## Safety and accountability
Never ask a user for their password so that you can sign in as them. Use only the website's authorized assume-identity feature. Do not make changes while impersonating unless the user-support task and your authorization require them.

If the website does not clearly show that impersonation is active, stop and contact program support rather than guessing.
