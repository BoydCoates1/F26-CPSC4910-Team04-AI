# Managing Sponsors

## Overview
Administrators can review and update sponsor information and manage sponsor users. They can also add sponsor users and manage other platform accounts as allowed by their role.

## Reviewing a sponsor
1. Sign in as an administrator.
2. Open the sponsor-management area.
3. Find and select the sponsor.
4. Review the information shown.

[CONFIRM IN WEBSITE: Sponsor search, fields, and navigation.]

## Updating sponsor information
Review the sponsor record, make the authorized changes, save, and verify the updated information.

Take care when changing sponsor details because drivers, sponsor users, catalogs, and reports may be associated with that organization.

## Sponsor users
Administrators can review or update sponsor users as part of user management. Sponsor organizations may also create additional sponsor users through their own self-management features.

## Sponsor-specific data
A sponsor's drivers, product catalog, and sponsor reports are organization-specific. Do not assume that changing one sponsor's settings should affect another sponsor.

## If a sponsor needs help
Direct sponsor users to the relevant sponsor help document. If the issue concerns platform-wide access or a capability unavailable to the sponsor, use the program's administrator or support process.
