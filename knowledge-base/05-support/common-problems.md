# Common Problems

This guide covers common user questions. The exact cause may depend on the current website state and the user's role.

## I cannot see a feature
- Confirm that you are signed in.
- Confirm that you are using the correct account and role.
- Check the available navigation and account permissions.
- Contact your sponsor administrator or program support if the feature should be available.

## My point balance did not change
- Refresh the page and review the point history.
- Check whether the change is still pending or whether a date filter is hiding it.
- Confirm the amount and reason with the sponsor.
- Ask an authorized sponsor user to investigate if the change is missing or incorrect.

## A product is unavailable
Product availability can change at the third-party provider. Refresh the catalog and check the current availability and points required. If the product remains unavailable, choose another available product or contact the sponsor.

## The points required changed
Product prices and availability are expected to be updated through a third-party product API. Review the current amount shown when adding the product to the cart or purchase set.

## I cannot place an order
Check:
- Whether the product is currently available.
- Whether you have enough points.
- Whether the product is in your sponsor's catalog.
- Whether the website displays a validation or error message.

If the issue continues, record the message and contact support. Do not send passwords or payment credentials.

## I cannot cancel or update an order
The available actions may depend on the order's current status and whether third-party fulfillment has started. Review the order details and contact the sponsor or support if no action is available.

## My order has not arrived
Review the order status and any tracking number shown. Because products are fulfilled by a third party, follow the provider's or program's stated delivery-support process.

## My application is still pending
The sponsor decides whether to accept or reject a driver application. Contact the sponsor if you need an update. Submitting an application does not guarantee acceptance.

## I received an unexpected notification
Review the related application, points, or order information in the website. If the notification appears incorrect, contact the sponsor or program support.

## What information should I provide to support?
Provide a short description of the issue, the page or action involved, the approximate date and time, and any non-sensitive error message. Do not provide passwords, reset codes, API keys, or full private account information.
