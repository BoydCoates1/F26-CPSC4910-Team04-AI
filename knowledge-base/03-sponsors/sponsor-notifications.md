# Sponsor Notifications

Sponsor notification preferences include applications, purchases, and negative balances.

To view notifications, select the bell in the main navigation. To manage preferences, open the profile menu, choose **Settings**, then **Notification settings**.

If an expected notification does not appear, check the relevant application, purchase, or balance information and review the preference setting. Do not assume a particular delivery channel.

Do not promise that a disabled category will generate a notification.
