# Managing Driver Points

## Who can change points?
Authorized sponsor users can add or deduct points from drivers associated with their sponsor. Drivers can review their point balance and history but cannot award themselves points.

## Making a point adjustment
1. Sign in as a sponsor user and open **Dashboard**.
2. Find the driver in the **Drivers** section.
3. Use the point-change control for that driver.
4. Enter the requested amount and reason, then review and confirm the change.

The website's point-change flow records a reason. Make it specific and factual.

## Recurring or automatic awards
The **Montly Points** dashboard quick action (spelled that way in the interface) opens monthly point settings. The setup describes recurring points for active drivers on the first of the month, in Eastern time, with a configurable amount. Verify the current setting before telling anyone that an award is enabled or will run.

## Reviewing point history
Use the sponsor **Report** quick action to open **Driver Point Tracking**. It provides driver and date filters for reviewing point activity. Do not promise export or fields beyond what the report displays.

## Good practice
- Verify the correct driver before changing points.
- Use a specific, factual reason.
- Check the amount before confirming.
- Review the resulting balance or history.
- Never share or change a driver's password to perform a point adjustment.
