# Managing Drivers and Driver Applications

## Overview
Sponsors control which drivers they accept into their program. A driver application is a request for affiliation with the sponsor. Sponsor users can review applications and approve or reject them.

## Reviewing applications
1. Sign in as a sponsor user and open **Dashboard**.
2. In the **Drivers** section, select the applications icon to open **Applications**.
3. Review the application and use the available accept or reject action.
4. Follow the confirmation and any reason prompt shown by the website.

## Approving an application
Approval indicates that the sponsor accepts the driver into its program. The driver should then be associated with the sponsor, subject to the website's confirmation and account workflow.

## Rejecting an application
Rejecting an application means the sponsor does not accept the driver at that time. If the website asks for a reason, provide a clear and appropriate explanation.

## Finding applicants
The dashboard's **Drivers** section provides driver search and filters. Do not assume that the **Applications** page has every applicant sorting or search feature unless it is visible there.

## Managing participating drivers
Use **Dashboard** → **Drivers** to search and filter drivers associated with your sponsor. The section provides point-change and removal controls and an account-creation control. Use only the controls shown for the selected driver.

## Driver removal
Before removing a driver, review the website's confirmation and consequences. The project brief requires a non-disableable removal alert, but the current driver notification settings group removal notifications under the configurable **Applications** category. Do not claim that the alert is guaranteed if that preference is disabled.

## Privacy
Only access applicant and driver information for legitimate sponsor-program purposes. Do not share personal information outside authorized channels.
