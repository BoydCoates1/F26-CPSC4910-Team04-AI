# Managing Drivers and Driver Applications

## Overview
Sponsors control which drivers they accept into their program. A driver application is a request for affiliation with the sponsor. Sponsor users can review applications and approve or reject them.

## Reviewing applications
1. Sign in as a sponsor user.
2. Open the driver applications or applicant-management area.
3. Review the applicant information shown, such as name and email.
4. Open an application to review any additional details.
5. Choose the available approval or rejection action.
6. Provide a reason if the website requests one and confirm the decision.

## Approving an application
Approval indicates that the sponsor accepts the driver into its program. The driver should then be associated with the sponsor, subject to the website's confirmation and account workflow.

## Rejecting an application
Rejecting an application means the sponsor does not accept the driver at that time. If the website asks for a reason, provide a clear and appropriate explanation.

## Finding applicants
The project work items mention viewing applicant names and email addresses and searching or ordering applicants by name. These details should be treated as planned or requested behavior until confirmed in the live website.

## Managing participating drivers
Sponsor users are intended to be able to review participating drivers, view relevant driver information and status, and add or update driver information.

Use the sponsor's driver-management area and select the relevant driver. Only manage drivers associated with your sponsor.

## Driver removal
The project requirements include an alert to a driver when a sponsor or administrator drops them. Before removing a driver, review the website's confirmation message and any consequences it describes. Do not assume removal can be reversed or that the driver retains access.

## Privacy
Only access applicant and driver information for legitimate sponsor-program purposes. Do not share personal information outside authorized channels.
